### [Yasu: Stress & HRV for Watch](https://yasu.raylabs.io)

**See your stress. Bring it down.** Yasu turns the heart readings your Apple Watch already takes into one number, measured against your own usual. When it rises, one tap says what it was. When it is high, 90 seconds of paced breathing helps bring it down. Your health data stays on your devices.

### [Lumical: Scan to Calendar](https://lumical.app)

**Scan your whole schedule at once.** Photograph a paper roster, a meeting invite, a poster or a flyer, and Lumical turns it into calendar events in seconds, with no typing. Review every event, then save them all to Apple Calendar with one tap. When the schedule changes, scan it again and only what moved is updated.
